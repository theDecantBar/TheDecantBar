## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: realistic transparent smoke overlay for an animated luxury perfume website. Generate ONLY delicate golden amber smoke / incense fumes, isolated on a genuinely transparent background with alpha. Wide landscape composition 1536x1024. Intricately detailed translucent curling wisps and thin luminous ribbons, natural turbulent vortices and soft billowing tendrils, warm gold rim light, rich realistic volumetric photography quality. Smoke flows horizontally along the lower third then curls upward at both sides with several elegant rising whorls. Plenty of transparent negative space in the top middle. All edges fade gradually to fully transparent, no rectangular bounds or cut off tendrils. No perfume bottle, no pedestal, no water, no ground, no objects, no text, no logos, no solid or black background. Must look like physically real backlit incense smoke in a high-end black and gold perfume commercial; airy detailed filaments, not opaque cotton clouds.

## Motion
